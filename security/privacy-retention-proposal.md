## Deletion implementation and verification (required before launch)
**Engineer**: schema separates `extraction_private`, `check_private`, `jobs`, dedup/quota metadata and public-safe snapshots (names illustrative, not existing tables). Add required retention class, created_at/expires_at, narrow read/delete functions and indexes. No opaque JSON blob containing complete OCR or request data. RLS deny direct anon access; capability checks server-side on every read/write/delete. No row created without expiry.

Use **one primary scheduled deletion mechanism**: Supabase `pg_cron` every15min if approved/available, otherwise a Cloudflare Cron Trigger calling a narrow server-only cleanup function. Verify plan/extension support first; neither is configured here. Atomic bounded batches, retry-safe cascade and orphan sweeps; deletion metrics contain counts/oldest overdue age, not payload. Daily job covers evidence/licence expiry and log/aggregate TTLs. Avoid competing untracked cron jobs. No Storage lifecycle is relied on because no image bucket exists. If storage is subsequently approved, remove objects through the Storage API and orphan sweep, not SQL metadata deletion alone; scheduled lifecycle availability/cost must be verified.

Reads deny at `now >= expires_at` even during scheduler outage. Normal-operation deletion objective: private/share payload removed within15min of expiry; alert SecOps after one missed run/overdue>15min, investigate and pause new affected server-side check/share creation if unresolved>1h (remote extraction is already OFF). This is an operational objective, not a physical-erasure guarantee during provider outage. DB free-plan pause can suspend cleanup; on resumption purge overdue rows **before reopening access**. Never expose expired data while catching up.

Required QA evidence (not yet run): insert synthetic due/expired/live rows, advance test clock, verify API+DB-role denial at exact expiry, execute cleanup and count all child rows, verify HTML+PNG revoke/expiry, cancel and manual delete, fail scheduler then recover, prove private-field removal while independent sanitised share persists. Verify no PII in traces/build artifacts and that local Clear removes Recent only. Alert on drift between notice and configuration.

### Backups, regions and processors
Select Supabase region and confirm Cloudflare/Supabase processing/cross-border locations; no Pakistan-only residency promise. Gemini is OFF. Distinguish **immediate access denial**, **active-row removal**, **customer recovery-backup retention**, and **physical erasure of all processor copies**. One is not proof of the others.

Official [Supabase backups](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups) and [Free backup FAQ](https://supabase.com/kb/faqs/platform/free-plan-backups) opened9 Nov2026 PKT: Free has **no automatic customer backups**; manual CLI exports are possible. Pro includes daily backups with a **7-day customer recovery window**; Team14d, Enterprise up to30d. Paid PITR uses physical snapshots+WAL, replaces daily backups, and offers7/14/28-day windows (~$100/$200/$400 per month add-on, plus plan/compute; all need Founder approval). A7-day restore window is **not a documented promise to physically erase every processor copy within7 days**, and deleting a row does not rewrite existing snapshots/WAL. Free's lack of automatic customer backups is not proof of no internal redundancy/recovery remnants or zero retention. No account settings were inspected.

Recommended Free launch: no app-created exports of transient/check/share rows; back up only reviewed schema/config and rebuildable rights-cleared catalogue if approved, avoiding token/private payload leakage. Founder must explicitly accept data-loss/rebuild risk or approve a different recovery plan. If app-controlled encrypted user-content backups are approved, configure and test rotation≤7d after active deletion, all replicas/exports included, restricted operators; do not assert managed physical-backup per-table exclusion is possible. For managed Pro/PITR, disclose chosen recovery window and obtain provider/DPA clarification of underlying deletion before promising a physical-erasure number. Changing plans requires privacy/recovery re-review. Necessary legal holds need documented scope/owner/review date, not blanket indefinite retention.

Restore **quarantined/offline**. Purge expired rows before serving and replay deletions/revocations occurring after the backup. Expiry alone does not prevent resurrection of an unexpired check deleted yesterday or a revoked30-day share restored from last week's backup. Require a minimal restricted deletion/revocation journal independent of that rollback (retained only through the last backup capable of resurrection, Founder-approved TTL), or fail closed by invalidating all restored private capabilities and shares if reconciliation cannot be proven. A journal in the same restored DB is insufficient. Never restore old signing/deploy secrets blindly. Test this scenario before launch; no restore/rehearsal is claimed.

Cloudflare/Supabase may retain network/security/billing metadata under contracts. App-log7d/security-audit30d are **maximum schedules**, not guaranteed availability on free plans. [Workers Logs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/observability/logs/workers-logs/) currently documents3d Free/7d Paid and automatic invocation logs with request URLs; future pricing changes require review. Disable URL-bearing invocation logging where needed and use approved route-template events; verify Supabase gateway/Postgres/error sinks too. Application console redaction does not scrub provider access logs. Inventory all actual sink TTLs/regions/settings and disclose processor exceptions. No marketing analytics/session replay/ad tracking or training corpus.

**User backup copy, until actual plan/config verified:** “Expiry or deletion stops access through Kitne Ka and our scheduled cleanup removes active records. Restricted recovery copies may remain under our hosting provider's backup cycle; we prevent deleted or revoked data from becoming accessible after restoration. We do not promise immediate physical erasure of every provider copy.” Add a precise recovery-window sentence only once chosen and verified: e.g. for Pro, “Our database service provides seven days of recovery backups, which may temporarily contain deleted records.” **Do not say “all backups roll off within7 days” or any universal N-day physical-erasure guarantee on current evidence.**

That parliamentary record is not the same thing as final resolution of every government draft.

No enactment was verified in this review; absence of an observed Act is not definitive legal advice or a complete Gazette search. Obtain counsel confirmation before launch and recheck at G4. Do not present draft localisation, regulator registration, consent or breach-notification provisions as current universal statutory duties. Existing constitutional privacy principles (Article14), PECA, contractual/confidentiality and consumer obligations may still apply; scope and cross-border requirements require qualified legal review. A bill's draft status is **not** permission to collect unnecessary personal data.

Adopt purpose limitation, minimisation, explicit remote/share choice, secure default access, short TTLs, deletion, accurate disclosure, processor oversight and a documented incident process regardless of bill status. Do not advertise “GDPR compliant”, certified privacy or Pakistan-only processing without substantiation. Public share consent is separate from extraction consent; uploader permission is not the depicted third party's consent or source reuse licence.
